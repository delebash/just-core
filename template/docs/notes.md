# Notes

Notes is the app's example area: short notes, kept by the app's server.

## Writing a note

Press **New note**, give it a title and write the note. A title can be up to 120 characters and
a note up to 20,000 — the form tells you under the field when something is too long or missing.
**Pin to the top of the list** keeps a note above the others.

## Suggest a title

**Suggest a title** asks the AI for a short title from what the note says and puts it in the
Title field — change it as you like before saving. It needs a note to read, and the AI set up
(see [Set up the AI](ai-providers.md)). While it runs, the button waits and the **AI tasks** panel
shows the progress; a failure shows there too, with **Retry**.

## Finding and changing notes

Pinned notes come first, then the most recently changed. **Filter notes** narrows the list to
notes whose title or text contains what you type. Click a note to edit it; the pencil does the
same, and the bin deletes it after asking — a deleted note can't be brought back except from a
backup (**Settings → Backups**).

## Notes per page

**Settings → General → Notes per page** sets how many notes a page of the list shows (5 to 100).
